An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition
[news-image-20261124](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124):
sum up 24 November 2026's world news in one image.

## The image

The entry is a newspaper front page headlined **"TUESDAY 24 NOVEMBER 2026"**,
with four stories from the day:

1. **COP31 climate talks collapse** — the climate summit ends without a deal.
2. **Russian warship hit by sea drone at Benghazi** — a Russian warship is
   struck by a sea drone off the Libyan port of Benghazi.
3. **Russian strikes kill 15 in Kyiv region** — Russian strikes kill fifteen
   people in the Kyiv region.
4. **Democrats' biggest US House majority in decades** — the Democrats win
   their largest majority in the US House of Representatives in decades.

A footer story covers Nvidia reinstating its CEO.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to the output
directory given by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
